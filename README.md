# Tank 'n' Roll -- Sprint 5

This repository contains the project developed during the fifth sprint
at **Epitech Berlin**, where we were tasked with creating a multiplayer game.

## 🎮 Gameplay & Mechanics

Tank 'n' Roll is a top-down multiplayer tank game inspired by Wii Tanks, supporting up to 4 players on a single map.
 
### Core Gameplay
 
Players choose from 12 unique tanks and battle across 6 handcrafted maps. The goal is simple, **be the last tank standing**. All tanks die in a single hit, so positioning and awareness are everything.
 
### Tank Variety
 
Each of the 12 tanks has its own stat profile, differing in:
 
- **Shell type:** Bouncy shells ricochet off walls, missiles travel fast in a straight line, and mortars arc through the air and explode in an area on impact
- **Movement speed:** ranging from slow and heavy to fast and agile
- **Active shell limit:** how many shells can exist in the world at once before you must wait
- **Bomb capacity:** how many landmines can be placed simultaneously
### Landmines
 
Players can place landmines beneath their tank. Once the player moves off the mine it begins a 1 second arming delay, after which it becomes active. Any tank that drives over an armed mine triggers an area explosion. Mines have a 14 second lifetime, if not triggered they explode on their own, so placement is a risk as much as a strategy.
 
### Maps
 
7 maps of varying sizes and layouts, featuring both full walls that block movement and shells, and low walls that block movement but can be shot over, encouraging creative use of bouncing shells and mortar arcs.
