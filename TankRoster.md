## 🛡️ Tank Roster

Each tank has a unique combination of shell type, movement speed, active shell limit and bomb limit. Choose the one that fits your playstyle.

---

### 🔵 Blue Team

| Tank | Shell Type | Movement Speed | Shell Limit | Bomb Limit |
|------|-----------|---------------|-------------|------------|
| Blue 1 | Bouncy | 600 | 4 | 2 |
| Blue 2 | None | 900 | 0 | 6 |
| Blue 3 | Missile + Mortar | 500 | 3 missiles | 2 mortars |

> **Blue 2** forgoes shells entirely in favour of speed and an arsenal of landmines.
> **Blue 3** is the only tank that carries two shell types simultaneously — missiles for direct combat and mortars for area denial.

---

### 🟢 Green Team

| Tank | Shell Type | Movement Speed | Shell Limit | Bomb Limit |
|------|-----------|---------------|-------------|------------|
| Green 1 | Missile | 700 | 3 | 3 |
| Green 2 | Bouncy | 650 | 4 | 2 |
| Green 3 | Bouncy | 400 | 8 | 2 |

> **Green 3** trades speed for firepower — the highest shell limit of any bouncy tank at the cost of being the slowest in the green lineup.

---

### ⚪ White Team

| Tank | Shell Type | Movement Speed | Shell Limit | Bomb Limit |

### 🟡 Yellow Team

| Tank | Shell Type | Movement Speed | Shell Limit | Bomb Limit |
|------|-----------|---------------|-------------|------------|
| Yellow 1 | Missile | 600 | 3 | 3 |
